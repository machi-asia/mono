## Description

**\`Support Link: Internal**
**Client Name/ Store Link: Fruitgals**

**Description: 7/11 attack**

Percentage: 98%

Some broken images were left alone to save time
*   Unpublished product images, these products will not appear in customer side
*   Uncategorized products, due to the lack of information on whether a product was local or imported, some products were left uncategorized, and therefore unable to fix the product images since our setup doesn't allow to save images with a missing product category

Some images were not recovered and was instead extracted from google
*   [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1i22bh092ZWyCTQBC89sW1bjoDhnMkHqYVrr2Rc0LHV8/edit?usp=drivesdk](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1i22bh092ZWyCTQBC89sW1bjoDhnMkHqYVrr2Rc0LHV8/edit?usp=drivesdk)

Due to this, some images were pulled out from the media library to prevent clients from being misinformed with mismatched product visuals. These will be provided and fixes by the client themselves
*   Boxes and Baskets
*   Specific fruit visuals

When the website was found, it only had a product listing page with no other page whatsoever. To fix this, I make the home page from the ground up using the [https://www.figma.com/design/vh3GLUINBRXbZAW1Sv2mvU/Fruitgals?node-id=0-1&p=f&t=owI4Qg0Pex8oSlvs-0](https://www.figma.com/design/vh3GLUINBRXbZAW1Sv2mvU/Fruitgals?node-id=0-1&p=f&t=owI4Qg0Pex8oSlvs-0)prototype as a reference. Three other pages were disregarded since it was a design to edit the single product pages, p1 page builder does not allow merchants to customize their single product pages

Issues observed:
*   Header expands upon scroll down, fix unknown as the image scaler in menu builder seems to not affect the enlarged logo
*   Both in the prototype and the original page, contact and about pages existed and was exposed to nav bar, but it does not exist in the prototype nor in the original site
